Sponsor Stack Menu

Help fund stack guides that developers and coding agents can read while building an application.

What sponsors get

Your logo appears in the Sponsored section of the homepage, with a dedicated profile that shows what your product does, where it fits and how to get started.

If your sponsorship includes agent guidance, we list your product as an optional add-on when it fits what the user is building. We put the label Paid sponsor of Stack Menu next to the product link.

How recommendations stay independent

We recommend a stack based on the project’s requirements and our research. A sponsorship does not change stack recommendations or their rankings.

See how it works with Bruno

Claude Code recommends a stack for a package-tracking site. When the site needs live data from a carrier, Claude suggests Bruno as an optional tool and labels it Paid sponsor of Stack Menu. After I choose Bruno, Claude creates an EasyPost collection and explains which test key it needs to run it. When staff update the tracking status by hand, Bruno stays out of the answer.
